# who

who launcher and the who apps. quiet, minimal, free. android only.

## apps

- who launcher — home, app list, search, notes, contacts, phone, messages, clock, calendar
- whoboard — keyboard
- whogram — quiet instagram
- who clock, who calendar, who notes, who contacts, who phone, who messages — standalone versions

## install

1. download the apk from [latest release](https://github.com/lollo259/who/releases/latest).
2. open it and allow installing from your browser or files app.
3. for notification access and accessibility: settings → apps → who launcher → ⋮ → allow restricted settings. then turn the permission on.
